Definitions, method and sources

Thermal Health Index (THI) — a 0–100 score per run: 50% peak score (100 at ≤ 35 °C, 0 at ≥ 60 °C), 20% exposure score (share of the run spent at or below 45 °C), 30% uniformity score (100 at 0 °C spread, 0 at ≥ 10 °C; single cells skip this part and the weights rescale). Thresholds live in the warehouse table dim_threshold.

Reconstruction — a lumped thermal model (cell node + enthalpy-method paraffin/copper-foam node). For each run one parameter is fitted so the simulated peak equals the published peak: heat generation for no-PCM single cells, then effective cooling or a thermal-path scale for the others; module runs also fit a cell-to-cell heat spread to match the published ΔT. Cell-to-composite conductance comes from the paper's measured foam conductivity (4.42 and 2.89 W/m·K; 20 PPI interpolated).

Limits — the published module tests had no 20/30 PPI peaks, so only 10 PPI is shown for the module. No published study matched a 3×2 or 4×2 layout with a full data set; the 2×2 and 4×4 modules are the closest. Ambient was not stated in Wang Z. et al. 2017, and its foam case was insulated while its baseline was open air.
